Install:

Place GroupCallouts.net.dll into the \callouts folder.

Callout Types:
- Group Fight (10 unarmed people fighting each other)
- Group Gun Fight (4 armed people fighting each other)
- Group Attack (3 armed people chasing and attacking 1 unarmed civilian)
